Vanished staff are now hidden from player lists and player counts, not only from the chat bridge.

### Fixed

- Each server marks the vanished players it can see on its player list and in its player count. The bot shows them only to people with the `minecraft.server.view_vanished` permission, in `/playerlist` and on the dashboard. Before this, vanished staff appeared there for everyone.
- The same vanish sources as the chat bridge: PremiumVanish on Paper, Spigot, Folia, Velocity and BungeeCord; SuperVanish on Paper and Spigot; EssentialsX, CMI and similar plugins on backends. A proxy sees vanish only through PremiumVanish's proxy half.
- On a proxy with PremiumVanish, a joining player is counted as hidden until PremiumVanish has decided, so a vanished player waiting in an auth lobby is not shown.
- Heimdall looks for a missing PremiumVanish at most every five seconds, rather than on every check.

Works with the current Heimdall bot release (1.85.0 and later); no bot update needed.
